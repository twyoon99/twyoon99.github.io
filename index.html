About

Hello! I am an M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence at Konkuk University, working in the NLP Lab. I received my B.S. in Medical Artificial Intelligence from Konyang University.

My work spans efficient and trustworthy LLM reasoning, from token- and activation-level confidence signals to adaptive inference methods, as well as multimodal evaluation. I previously worked as a research intern at ETRI and as an intern at TUNiB.
